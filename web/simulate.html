Methodology, plainly stated

Baseline annual collisions per state = real reported animal-collision total ÷ years covered by that state's source (see the Data page for which spans are confirmed vs. assumed). Collisions prevented = baseline × adoption rate × 50% alert effectiveness. Money saved uses ~$7,300/collision and injuries prevented uses a 2% injury rate — both derived from commonly cited national wildlife-vehicle collision figures (~1.3M collisions/yr, ~$9.5B/yr cost, ~26,000 injuries/yr), not measured directly in this dataset. The map's per-event animation samples real hotspots weighted by their actual historical crash count, so where events appear is grounded in real geography even though which exact collisions would be prevented is, necessarily, simulated.

Hotspot locations, severities, and per-state totals are real. Effectiveness, cost, and injury multipliers are cited estimates, not measurements — see the project README.
