Methodology

Baseline annual collisions per state = real reported total ÷ years covered by that state's source. Collisions prevented = baseline × adoption × 50% alert effectiveness. Money saved uses $6,000/collision; injuries prevented uses a 1.7% injury rate, both the midpoint of published national ranges. Map events sample real hotspots weighted by actual historical crash count.

Hotspot locations, severities, and per-state totals are real. Effectiveness, cost, and injury multipliers come from published national estimates. See the project README.
