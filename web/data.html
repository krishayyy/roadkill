Model performance — honest, not leaky

An earlier version of this model scored 0.867 ROC AUC using cluster-derived features to predict cluster-derived labels — a leak. After fixing it with proper spatial cross-validation (holding out entire geographic blocks, not random rows), the honest score is lower. That's the correct direction for fixing a leak, not a regression.

What the model actually relies on

Hour of day and month dominate — animal activity is strongly time-patterned (dawn/dusk, fall rut). Species features carry ~zero importance because none of the five source datasets have a reliable species field.

Full methodology, every known limitation, and the raw evaluation JSON: see scratchpad_data/training_evaluations_v3.json and the project README.
