Honest, not leaky

An earlier version scored 0.867 ROC AUC using cluster-derived features to predict cluster-derived labels — a leak. Spatial cross-validation, holding out entire geographic blocks, gives the honest number. Lower is correct here, not a regression.

What drives the prediction
