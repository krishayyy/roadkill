How the demo works

Baseline first, then inject the app.

The simulation isn't tuned to produce a nice-looking number. It's calibrated to match real, reported annual collision totals first — then a slider adds "safer drivers" on top, so every improvement shown is relative to something real.

1

Real hotspots, real severity

2,989 hotspots from DBSCAN clustering of real DOT animal-collision coordinates in IA, IL, VA, MA, and TN. Each hotspot's weight is its actual historical crash count.

2

Calibrated baseline

Each state's simulated annual collision rate is set to match its real reported total (annualized from the source's known date range) — not a guess, a fit.

3

Inject app adoption

Slide adoption from 0% to 100%. Alerted drivers avoid a collision at a rate grounded in published wildlife-warning-system field studies. Watch collisions, cost, injuries, and animals-saved update live on the real map.

Why it matters

Three numbers, tracked live in the simulation.

💵 Money saved

Every prevented collision avoids an average ~$7,300 in vehicle damage, medical, towing, and administrative cost (based on commonly cited national wildlife-collision cost estimates).

🩹 Injuries prevented

About 1 in 50 wildlife-vehicle collisions nationally results in a human injury. Fewer collisions means fewer injured drivers and passengers.

🦌 Animals saved

Directly from the real data: every prevented collision is one fewer animal struck, counted at the real hotspot where it would have happened.
